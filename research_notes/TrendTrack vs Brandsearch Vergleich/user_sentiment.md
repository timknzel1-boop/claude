# User sentiment and criticism: TrendTrack (trendtrack.io) vs Brandsearch (brandsearch.co)

Method note: research as of 2026-11-15. Direct fetches of trustpilot.com, brandsearch.co and winninghunter.com failed: the hosts are not allowed in this session's network settings. All findings below come from search-engine summaries and snippets, not from reading the pages in full. Review counts and scores therefore come from snapshots of different ages and do not agree. Search engines returned no Reddit threads for either tool.

## 1. Trustpilot ratings, review counts and negative-review themes

### Takeaway
Both tools have small, mostly 5-star Trustpilot profiles, and the snapshots disagree. TrendTrack has about 4.4–4.8/5 from 54–133 reviews. Brandsearch has about 4.1–4.6/5 from 18–21 reviews. The few negative reviews are about plan limits and AI-credit use (TrendTrack) and about support and ads not showing (Brandsearch).

### Cited Findings
**TrendTrack**
- Trustpilot (CA subdomain snapshot): 4.8/5, "Excellent", 56 reviews — [Trustpilot CA](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/trendtrack.io)
- Other snapshots conflict: one source cites 4.8/5 from 54 reviews, another cites 4.4/5 from 133 reviews — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/trendtrack.io); [trafik.plus](https://trafik.plus/blog/trendtrack-review-and-complete-guide) (as summarized by search)
- Positive themes: daily data refresh and accuracy ("Trendtrack is the only one with accurate and updated daily datas", from a reviewer who had tried several spy tools), responsive support, and frequent new features — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/trendtrack.io)
- Main negative review: the user bought the $59 Starter plan to use TrendTrack with Claude via MCP. After paying, they learned that MCP/API access needs Pro. They say the YouTube video that introduced them to the tool did not mention this, and they asked for a refund at once. They also report that three searches through Claude used about 10,000 AI API credits — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/trendtrack.io)
- TrendTrack's own pricing comparison confirms that API/MCP is "Not included" on Starter, with 10,000 credits starting at Pro — [TrendTrack blog](https://www.trendtrack.io/blog-post/trendtrack-review-2026). storeverdict.com wrongly says API/MCP is on every plan — [storeverdict](https://storeverdict.com/trendtrack-api/)
- The search summary says the company has not replied to negative reviews on Trustpilot — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/trendtrack.io)
- Cost is "the most common friction point raised by users" — [GetHookd (competitor)](https://www.gethookd.ai/learn/trendtrack-reviews-pricing-features-is-this-ecommerce-intelligence-tool-legit/)

**Brandsearch**
- Trustpilot snapshots: 4.1/5 "Great" from 21 reviews (metadata suggests this snapshot may be old), and 4.5/5 from 18 reviews on AU/NZ pages — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/brandsearch.co); [Trustpilot AU](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/brandsearch.co); [isitgooddeal](https://www.isitgooddeal.com/is-brandsearch-a-good-deal/)
- WinningHunter (a competitor) cites 4.6/5 from 18 reviews, all published ratings at 5 stars, and calls this "early-stage traction, not long-term validation" — [WinningHunter](https://winninghunter.com/insights/brandsearch-review)
- Negative reviews found: a 2-star review titled "Customer service is terrible", and a 1-star review saying the ads don't show (the company replied with an apology). In another reply, the developer explained that traffic data comes from the Similarweb API and asked for examples of mismatched traffic — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/brandsearch.co); [WinningHunter](https://winninghunter.com/insights/brandsearch-review)
- Trustpilot says Brandsearch "hasn't invited customers recently" — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/brandsearch.co)
- WinningHunter found no G2 profile matching the G2 review volume shown on Brandsearch's own website — [WinningHunter](https://winninghunter.com/insights/brandsearch-review)

### Inferences
- Both samples are too small and too skewed toward 5 stars to rank the tools. TrendTrack's larger review count suggests a bigger or more review-solicited user base.
- The TrendTrack complaint is real but narrow. It concerns plan-tier transparency for MCP/API, which matters for AI-workflow users.
- Brandsearch's unverifiable G2 claim is a small trust red flag.

### Gaps
- Live current counts and full lists of 1–3 star reviews could not be read, because the Trustpilot host is blocked.
- No G2 or Capterra profiles with meaningful review volume were found for either tool. Sources say independent marketplace reviews are "notably scarce".

## 2. Practitioner discussion (Reddit, X, YouTube, German communities)

### Takeaway
I found almost no organic practitioner discussion. Searches returned no Reddit threads on either tool, no independent German reviews, and only vendor or creator posts on X and YouTube. Most of what ranks is SEO, affiliate or competitor content.

### Cited Findings
- No Reddit threads on trendtrack.io came up in searches — [search results incl. Semrush overview](https://sv.semrush.com/website/trendtrack.io/overview)
- For Brandsearch, WinningHunter notes "no detailed YouTube breakdowns from established ecommerce educators, no long-form Reddit case studies" and scores it 7.4/10 — [WinningHunter via Toolsplorer/search](https://winninghunter.com/insights/brandsearch-review)
- Brandsearch is built around Shopify stores, so it has little data on WooCommerce, Amazon, Etsy or custom-platform competitors — [Toolsplorer](https://toolsplorer.com/tool/brandsearch/)
- TrendTrack launch post on X by co-founder Vincent Alonzi (in French): it was "developed with founders of 7–8+ figure brands, media buyers and agencies" — [X @Bixentee_](https://x.com/Bixentee_/status/1910000095522587044)
- The YouTube channel matters for acquisition: the negative Trustpilot reviewer found TrendTrack through a YouTube video — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/trendtrack.io)
- Data caveats from reviews:
  - TrendTrack revenue figures are modeled estimates — [WinningHunter](https://winninghunter.com/insights/trendtrack-review); [PassiveKit](https://www.passivekit.com/trendtrack-chromeextension/)
  - Traffic data comes from SimilarWeb
  - Reach and ad-spend data is only available for EU/UK ads (the EU DSA transparency data) — [trafik.plus](https://trafik.plus/blog/trendtrack-review-and-complete-guide)
  - Outside the EU, how long an ad has run becomes the proxy for whether it is profitable — [Scribehow](https://scribehow.com/o/_oQd9ajpQCmlQ1hAirU9PQ/page/TrendTrack_Review__Is_It_Worth_It_for_eCom_Store_Owners__3CXF5PKISdW7EOw49HD_TQ)
- EU/German relevance: one review notes a French-origin interface and a European-market data focus. TrendTrack publishes German-language blog posts (/de/) — [isitgooddeal](https://www.isitgooddeal.com/marketing-saas/ad-spy/); [TrendTrack DE blog](https://www.trendtrack.io/de/blog-post/trendtrack-vs-gethookd)
- One 2026 comparison says TrendTrack lacks TikTok ad coverage and recommends pairing a store-focused tool with an ad-focused one — [trendtrackpromocode.com (affiliate)](https://trendtrackpromocode.com/blog/best-shopify-spy-tools/). This conflicts with TrendTrack's own claim of a TikTok feature (see section 4)
- One comparison site scores BrandSearch 7.6 and TrendTrack 7.4 — [isitgooddeal](https://www.isitgooddeal.com/marketing-saas/ad-spy/)

### Inferences
- For EU/German buyers, TrendTrack's EU-spend data is a real advantage, because the DSA makes reach and spend visible for EU ads.
- Both tools seem used mainly by Shopify store owners and dropshippers for store and product research, not by in-house creative teams.

### Gaps
- No genuine Reddit, German forum or X threads from independent media buyers were found for either tool. This silence is itself a signal that neither tool is widely discussed.

## 3. Billing and trial issues

### Takeaway
I found no documented reports of Brandsearch charging users after they cancelled the $1 trial. The structure, though, is a standard paid trial that converts automatically with no refunds. TrendTrack has no clear free trial and no blanket money-back guarantee.

### Cited Findings
- Brandsearch offers a 72-hour trial pass for $1.00 — [isitgooddeal](https://www.isitgooddeal.com/is-brandsearch-a-good-deal/); [brandsearchreview.com](https://brandsearchreview.com/brandsearch-review/)
- Trial terms (per an affiliate guide): a card is required, the $1 is a real charge rather than a $0 authorization, and the trial converts to the selected plan unless cancelled before the charge — [brandsearchreview.pro](https://brandsearchreview.pro/blog/brandsearch-free-trial/)
- Brandsearch terms:
  - Subscription with recurring charges
  - Payments non-refundable except as required by law
  - No refund for unused periods
  - Cancellation any time with no fee; access continues to the end of the paid period
  — [Brandsearch legal](https://brandsearch.co/legal); [Pricing](https://brandsearch.co/pricing)
- Brandsearch pricing cited at about $63/mo or €79+/mo — [isitgooddeal](https://www.isitgooddeal.com/marketing-saas/ad-spy/)
- TrendTrack does not advertise a blanket money-back guarantee — [trendtrackreview.com (affiliate)](https://trendtrackreview.com/free-trial)
- TrendTrack's free-trial situation is unclear ("no clear free trial") — search summary of [trendtrackreview.com](https://trendtrackreview.com/)
- TrendTrack prices are inconsistent across sources:
  - €59/€89/€149 (Starter/Professional/Advanced) — [TrendTrack mission](https://www.trendtrack.io/mission)
  - $59/$89/$149 — [trafik.plus](https://trafik.plus/blog/trendtrack-review-and-complete-guide)
  - "from $42/mo" on annual billing — [trendtrackreview.com/pricing](https://trendtrackreview.com/pricing)

### Inferences
- The risk with Brandsearch is the usual auto-renewal trap: you must cancel within 72 hours, and the next charge is not refunded.
- With TrendTrack, buyers should check which tier includes which features (especially API/MCP) before paying, since refunds are not guaranteed.

### Gaps
- No Reddit or Trustpilot posts were found that document disputed Brandsearch trial charges. I could not check the full Trustpilot text.

## 4. Company background and development activity

### Takeaway
TrendTrack is a French-founded startup (CEO and co-founder Vincent Alonzi), founded around Dec 2024, launched publicly around April 2025, with no known external funding. It ships features actively, including API/MCP in April 2026 and TikTok coverage. Brandsearch is run by a Luxembourg company, SPECTRE PRYSM S.à r.l.-S, with no publicly named founder. Its "Spectre AI" is an AI tracking feature, not a separate company.

### Cited Findings
**TrendTrack**
- CEO and co-founder Vincent Alonzi announced the launch on X in French, around April 2025 (status ID 1910000095522587044) — [X](https://x.com/Bixentee_/status/1910000095522587044)
- Dealroom: founded Dec 2024, 11–50 employees, no known external funding — [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/trendtrack)
- The Chrome Web Store developer is "Sendcorp", 60 Rue François 1er, 75008 Paris, contact@trendtrack.io — [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/trendtrack-ecommerce-anal/cncdmciajmaojninbopinpijdodjgfpd)
- The Trustpilot profile lists a Dubai entity, "HVSCALE SERVICES - FZCO", which conflicts with the Paris listing — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/trendtrack.io) (via search summary)
- Not to be confused with Trendtracker, a Belgian AI strategy company in Ghent that raised €5.9M — [EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2025/12/trendtracker-raises-9-million-scale-ai-driven-strategic-intelligence-platform-globally/)
- April 27, 2026: "TrendTrack API & MCP are live", for connecting Claude, GPT and Gemini — [X @trendtrack_io](https://x.com/trendtrack_io/status/2048801022731026791)
- TikTok feature, vendor claims: 95M+ TikToks indexed, 700k+ brand profiles, 24h refresh, including brands' organic content — [TrendTrack blog](https://www.trendtrack.io/blog-post/what-is-the-best-ai-ad-spying-tool-for-tiktok-ads-in-e-commerce)
- Affiliate program: 30% lifetime recurring commission — [recurringcommission.com](https://recurringcommission.com/programs/trendtrack/)
- Semrush (July 2025): about 50% of traffic is direct, and traffic was down 38.7% compared with May 2025 — [Semrush](https://sv.semrush.com/website/trendtrack.io/overview)

**Brandsearch**
- Operator: SPECTRE PRYSM S.à r.l.-S, Wemperhardt, Luxembourg — [Brandsearch legal](https://brandsearch.co/legal)
- The site credits "the BrandSearch team" as founder; no named person — search summary of brandsearch.co
- Spectre AI is automated competitor tracking with daily updates on creatives, funnels and promotions — [Toolsplorer](https://toolsplorer.com/tool/brandsearch/)
- Coverage: indexes Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Reels. Google ads are not indexed — [Brandsearch discovery](https://brandsearch.co/discovery)
- Vendor database figures are inconsistent: "160M+ active ads across 7.5M+ Shopify stores" vs "14M+ brands / 13.7M stores (9M Shopify)" — search summary of brandsearch.co pages / [Toolsplorer](https://toolsplorer.com/tool/brandsearch/)

### Inferences
- TrendTrack is bootstrapped and growth-marketing driven: affiliates, YouTube, and multilingual SEO blogs in DE, FR, IT, ES, NL, PT and ZH. Development is visibly active in 2026.
- Brandsearch's anonymous ownership through a Luxembourg single-person S.à r.l.-S lowers transparency.

### Gaps
- Funding is unverified for both, beyond Dealroom saying "no known funding" for TrendTrack. The Paris (Sendcorp) vs Dubai (FZCO) entity question is unresolved. No Brandsearch founding date or changelog was found.

## 6. Alternatives practitioners mention and when they prefer them

### Takeaway
2026 comparison guides split the market by use case:
- Foreplay for creative teams' swipe files
- Atria for analyzing your own ad accounts
- Minea for dropshipping product hunting
- Winning Hunter as the closest like-for-like alternative to TrendTrack
- Meta Ad Library as the free baseline

### Cited Findings
- Foreplay is the "leading default in 2026 for swipe files", about $59/mo entry. Spyder (competitor tracking) and Lens (analytics) start at about $175/mo — [superscale.ai and others](https://superscale.ai/learn/digital-ad-intelligence/)
- Atria targets analysis of your own ad accounts, with a Core plan at $129/mo billed annually with 5 seats. Community sentiment is split; some find its library less refined than Foreplay's — [comparison guides](https://www.quickads.ai/best-ad-spy-tools)
- Minea targets ecommerce and dropshipping product research, from about $49/mo. TikTok and Pinterest are reportedly still "coming soon" — [comparison guides](https://www.sprites.ai/blog/facebook-ad-spy-tool)
- Winning Hunter costs $49–249/mo with no credits, and is called "the closest like-for-like alternative to TrendTrack" — [comparison guide](https://ad-radar.dev/blog/best-ad-spy-tools)
- One guide recommends pairing a store-focused tool (TrendTrack or Brandsearch) with an ad-focused one — [trendtrackpromocode.com (affiliate)](https://trendtrackpromocode.com/blog/best-shopify-spy-tools/)

### Inferences
- Media buyers and creative strategists at DTC brands seem to prefer Foreplay or Atria for creative workflows. TrendTrack and Brandsearch serve store, product and competitor research, especially for Shopify sellers. Meta Ad Library (free, and showing EU reach under the DSA) covers basic needs for EU-focused buyers.

### Gaps
- No Reddit threads comparing these tools directly were retrieved. Statements like "Reddit users compare Foreplay and Atria most" come from blog guides and are not verified.
