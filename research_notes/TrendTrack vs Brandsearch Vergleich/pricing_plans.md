# TrendTrack vs Brandsearch: Pricing, Plans, Limits & Trial Terms (as of Nov 2026)

**Method note / data-quality warning:** Direct fetching was blocked in this session. trendtrack.io, brandsearch.co, ecomradar.co, trendtrackreview.com, passivekit.com and web.archive.org were all unreachable (DNS / blocked). Every finding below comes from **search-engine result snippets and summaries**, not from full page reads. Search snippets can be stale (the indexed copy of the official Brandsearch pricing page is marked "~652 days old"; the TrendTrack ToS snapshot is about 560 days old). Treat all numbers as "to verify on the live pricing page / checkout".

**Source trust ranking (high to low):**
1. Official vendor pages: trendtrack.io/pricing, trendtrack.io/legal/terms-of-service, the trendtrack.io FAQ, brandsearch.co/pricing, the brandsearch.co homepage and affiliate page. These are the most authoritative, but the vendor *blog posts* on both sites contradict their own pricing pages and some are outdated.
2. Dated, independent-ish review: ecomradar.co. It states it checked the TrendTrack pricing page on 18 Oct 2026, and its figures match the official yearly figures.
3. Affiliate or promo sites, which push discount codes and earn commissions: trendtrackreview.com, trendtrackpromocode.com, trendtrack.tech, passivekit.com, brandsearchreview.com, brandsearchreview.pro, promizi.com, toolsplorer.com (a directory), isitgooddeal.com, scribehow.com. 4. Competitor-written comparisons, which have a conflict of interest: winninghunter.com (a WinningHunter competitor), gethookd.ai (a GetHookd competitor), adspo.co, adnova.ai, boostecom.app, and trafik.plus (an agency/tool blog).

## Q1: What plans exist for each tool and what do they cost (monthly and annual)?

### Takeaway
TrendTrack has three self-serve plans (Starter / Pro / Business) plus a custom-priced Enterprise plan, billed monthly, quarterly or yearly. The most consistent list prices are $59 / $89 / $149 per month. The official pricing page (as indexed) shows yearly-billed rates of $34 / $62 / $111 per month, and ecomradar shows the same figures in € (checked 18 Oct 2026). Brandsearch has three plans: Starter $63/mo, Outscaler $79/mo and Agency $149/mo, with monthly or annual billing. Annual billing is "20% cheaper" according to the official site, while one affiliate review shows about 25% off ($568 / $712 / $1,432 per year).

### Cited Findings

**TrendTrack**
- Official pricing page (as indexed by search): yearly-billed rates of **$34 Starter / $62 Pro / $111 Business per month**, with monthly, quarterly and yearly toggles — [trendtrack.io/pricing](https://www.trendtrack.io/pricing)
- Ecomradar reports **€34 / €62 / €111 per month billed annually**, from the pricing page it checked on 18 Oct 2026 — [ecomradar.co](https://ecomradar.co/en/competitor-intel/trendtrack-review-pricing/). The same figures appear in USD on [trafik.plus](https://trafik.plus/blog/trendtrack-review-and-complete-guide). The currency is ambiguous: the site may show € or $ by geolocation, and this was not confirmed.
- The most common monthly list prices are **Starter $59, Pro $89, Business $149**. Annual billing is about **$42 / $63 / $105 per month**, with quarterly billing about 15% off and annual about 30% off — [trendtrackreview.com/pricing](https://trendtrackreview.com/pricing) (affiliate); [passivekit.com](https://www.passivekit.com/trendtrack-pricing/) (affiliate)
- TrendTrack's own blog contradicts itself:
  - The "Trendtrack Review 2026" post (about 47 days old at search time) lists **$49 / $89 / $159** monthly, with 15% off quarterly, 30% off annual, and custom-priced Enterprise — [trendtrack.io blog](https://www.trendtrack.io/blog-post/trendtrack-review-2026)
  - Older posts (about 208 days old) list $47 / $71 / $119 per month on yearly billing, and $59 / $89 / $149 monthly — [trendtrack.io blog: best adspy tool](https://www.trendtrack.io/blog-post/best-adspy-tool); [Minea vs PiPiAds vs TrendTrack](https://www.trendtrack.io/blog-post/minea-vs-pipiads-vs-trendtrack)
  - The AI-tools post (about 165 days old) lists $70 / $100 / $170 — [trendtrack.io blog](https://www.trendtrack.io/blog-post/top-6-best-ai-tools-for-e-commerce)
  - The media-buyers use-case page lists **€59 / €89 / €149** per month — [trendtrack.io/use-cases/media-buyers](https://www.trendtrack.io/use-cases/media-buyers)
- Enterprise is custom-priced and includes unlimited users and product usage, a custom API contract, and a dedicated Slack channel with an account manager — [trendtrack.io blog](https://www.trendtrack.io/blog-post/trendtrack-review-2026); [trendtrackreview.com](https://trendtrackreview.com/pricing)
- A free account and a free Chrome extension exist, but there is no free paid-plan tier — [trendtrackreview.com](https://trendtrackreview.com/)

**Brandsearch**
- Plans are **Starter $63/mo, Outscaler $79/mo, Agency $149/mo** — [Brandsearch blog: vs Foreplay](https://brandsearch.co/blog/brandsearch-vs-foreplay); [vs Minea](https://brandsearch.co/blog/brandsearch-vs-minea); [affiliate page](https://brandsearch.co/affiliate) (Outscaler $79). The official pricing page snippet showed features but no dollar figures.
- Annual billing at **$568 / $712 / $1,432 per year**, which is about $47.33 / $59.33 / $119.33 per month — [brandsearchreview.com](https://brandsearchreview.com/brandsearch-review/) (affiliate)
- The homepage says "annual plans are **20% cheaper** than monthly" and include unlimited Spectre — [brandsearch.co](https://brandsearch.co/). This conflicts with a review's "25% annual discount" — [winninghunter.com](https://winninghunter.com/insights/brandsearch-review) (competitor)
- A competitor comparison lists Starter at $79, Outscaler at $99 and Agency at $200. This is unverified and likely wrong or outdated — [adnova.ai](https://www.adnova.ai/compare/adnova-vs-brandsearch)
- Coupon claims: BRND20 gives 20% off monthly ($50.40 / $63.20 / $119.20) per [brandsearchreview.com](https://brandsearchreview.com/brandsearch-review/), and WINNING gives 20% off the first payment per another review ([brandsearchreview.pro](https://brandsearchreview.pro/blog/brandsearch-free-trial/)). Both are affiliate claims and unconfirmed.
- Prices are in USD in all Brandsearch sources found.

### Inferences
- The TrendTrack figures $34 / $62 / $111 (official page, yearly) and $42 / $63 / $105 (affiliate sites, "annual about 30% off" of $59 / $89 / $149) cannot both be current. The official page plus ecomradar's dated 18 Oct 2026 check is the most trustworthy. This suggests a **pricing restructure during 2026**: Starter got cheaper and Business more expensive on annual billing compared with earlier versions. The blog's $49 / $89 / $159 monthly figures may be the current monthly list, which would make yearly about 30% off ($34 ≈ 49×0.7; $62 ≈ 89×0.7; $111 ≈ 159×0.7). **These numbers are internally consistent with a 30% annual discount**, so the current TrendTrack monthly list is most likely **$49 / $89 / $159 (or €)**, and **$59 / $89 / $149 is the older structure**.
- For Brandsearch, $63 / $79 / $149 is consistent across the vendor's own blog and affiliate page, which makes it fairly reliable. The annual figure is either about 20% off (official) or $568 / $712 / $1,432 (review). Note that $1,432 per year ($119.33/mo) is exactly 20% off $149, and $712 ($59.33/mo) is about 25% off $79.

### Gaps
- The live official pricing pages could not be read, so exact current monthly prices, currency handling (€ vs $) and VAT treatment were not verified.
- No dated official announcement of a TrendTrack or Brandsearch price change was found.

## Q2: What are the limits per plan (brand tracker slots, search quotas, seats, exports, API)?

### Takeaway
On TrendTrack, sources conflict on brand-tracker slots: the majority view is 2 / 30 / unlimited, while Radar Ecom and Adspo say 2 / 10 / 20. Pro has 1 seat and Business has 3 seats (extra seats $29/mo). API access is included on Pro and Business. Brandsearch's official pricing page is clearer: Starter has 5 tracked brands, 1 seat, 15k lookups/mo, 5k API/MCP calls and $3 of AI credits. Outscaler has 50 brands, 2 seats, unlimited lookups, 10k API calls and $20 of AI credits. Agency has 100 brands, 5 seats, 10k API calls and $40 of AI credits.

### Cited Findings

**TrendTrack**
- Brand Tracker, majority view: Starter 2 brands, Pro 30, Business unlimited — [trendtrackreview.com](https://trendtrackreview.com/pricing); [passivekit.com](https://www.passivekit.com/trendtrack-pricing/); [winninghunter.com](https://winninghunter.com/insights/winninghunter-vs-trendtrack)
- **Conflicting view:** Starter 2, **Pro 10, Business 20** — [ecomradar.co](https://ecomradar.co/en/competitor-intel/trendtrack-review-pricing/); [adspo.co](https://adspo.co/blog/trendtrack-alternatives). Because ecomradar is dated 18 Oct 2026, the 2 / 10 / 20 figures may reflect the newer plan structure. This is unresolved.
- Seats: Pro has 1 user. Business has **3 seats, extra seats $29/mo** — [trendtrackreview.com](https://trendtrackreview.com/pricing); [scribehow.com](https://scribehow.com/o/_oQd9ajpQCmlQ1hAirU9PQ/page/TrendTrack_Review__Is_It_Worth_It_for_eCom_Store_Owners__3CXF5PKISdW7EOw49HD_TQ); [adspo.co](https://adspo.co/blog/trendtrack-alternatives). This conflicts with **4 seats** in [gethookd.ai](https://www.gethookd.ai/learn/trendtrack-reviews-pricing-features-is-this-ecommerce-intelligence-tool-legit/) and with 4 seats plus $15 per extra seat in a GitHub promo page ([github.com/pin07416/TrendTrack](https://github.com/pin07416/TrendTrack)), which is low trust.
- Search and lookup quotas: one review says Pro is capped at 30 searches per week and Business has unlimited searches; another says Business removes the lookup cap; another notes a 10,000-store monthly limit on Starter — [trafik.plus](https://trafik.plus/blog/trendtrack-review-and-complete-guide); [trendtrackreview.com](https://trendtrackreview.com/pricing). The wording is inconsistent.
- API: Pro and Business include API access. Credits are 10,000 for each per one source and 20,000 per another. Rate limits are reportedly Pro 20 req/s and Business 40 req/s — [trendtrackreview.com](https://trendtrackreview.com/pricing); [gethookd.ai](https://www.gethookd.ai/learn/trendtrack-reviews-pricing-features-is-this-ecommerce-intelligence-tool-legit/)
- Exports: no plan-level export caps were found. A free Shopify Product Exporter tool exists — [trendtrackpromocode.com](https://trendtrackpromocode.com/blog/how-to-use-trendtrack/)

**Brandsearch (official pricing page, indexed snippet, possibly stale)**
- A "tracked brand" is a competitor that Spectre watches daily. Plans differ on tracked brands, seats, AI credits and API calls — [brandsearch.co/pricing](https://brandsearch.co/pricing)
- **Starter:** 5 Spectre brands, 1 seat, 15,000 Brand Library lookups/mo, 5,000 API/MCP calls/mo, $3 of AI credits — [brandsearch.co/pricing](https://brandsearch.co/pricing)
- **Outscaler:** 50 brands, 2 seats, unlimited Brand Library lookups, 10,000 API/MCP calls, $20 of AI credits — [brandsearch.co/pricing](https://brandsearch.co/pricing); [affiliate page](https://brandsearch.co/affiliate)
- **Agency:** 100 brands, 5 seats, 10,000 API/MCP calls, $40 of AI credits — [brandsearch.co/pricing](https://brandsearch.co/pricing)
- Annual plans include "unlimited Spectre" — [brandsearch.co](https://brandsearch.co/)
- API and MCP: 32 tools are available for workflows — [brandsearch.co/news](https://brandsearch.co/news/build-ecommerce-workflows-api-mcp)
- Unlimited brand swapping is claimed by a third party, which means the caps apply to how many brands are tracked at the same time. This is unconfirmed on the official site.

### Inferences
- Brandsearch gives far more tracking slots per dollar: 50 brands for $79 compared with TrendTrack Pro's 10–30 brands for $89.
- TrendTrack's limit figures are the least reliable part of this dataset and should be checked on the live page.

### Gaps
- No reliable export-volume limits were found for either tool.
- The TrendTrack Starter search quota was unclear.
- It is unknown whether Brandsearch's "unlimited Spectre" on annual plans overrides the per-plan brand caps.

## Q3: Trial terms, cancellation and refunds

### Takeaway
TrendTrack has **no free trial**, only an interactive demo plus a free account and Chrome extension. Its official policy is **no refunds**, apart from an "exceptional cases within 24 hours of first purchase" clause. Affiliate sites' claims of a 30-day money-back guarantee contradict the official policy. Brandsearch offers a **$1 / 3-day full-access trial** that requires a card and **auto-converts** to the selected paid plan unless cancelled. Cancellation works at any time with no fee and access runs to the end of the paid period. No refund policy is documented for annual plans.

### Cited Findings

**TrendTrack**
- The official FAQ says: "No, there's no free trial. However, you'll get instant access to a fully interactive demo" — [trendtrack.io](https://www.trendtrack.io/use-cases/dtc-owner)
- The official FAQ also says TrendTrack operates a no-refund policy. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. Yearly plans can be cancelled, but no refunds are issued after payment — [trendtrack.io](https://www.trendtrack.io/use-cases/dtc-owner)
- Terms of Service: cancelling stops renewal and access lasts through the paid period. Cancel **at least 24 hours before renewal** to avoid charges. Subscriptions are non-refundable except in exceptional, justified cases within 24 hours of a first-time purchase. The indexed ToS snapshot is about 560 days old — [trendtrack.io/legal/terms-of-service](https://www.trendtrack.io/legal/terms-of-service)
- Conflicting third-party claims of a "30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans" appear on [trendtrack.tech](https://trendtrack.tech/en/trendtrack-free-trial/) and [trendtrackpromocode.com](https://trendtrackpromocode.com/blog/trendtrack-free-trial/), both affiliate sites. These are **contradicted by the official FAQ and ToS**.

**Brandsearch**
- The official site advertises "$1 for 3 days (full access)" — [brandsearch.co](https://brandsearch.co/); [brandsearch.co/lp](https://brandsearch.co/lp)
- A card is required and the trial converts to a paid plan if not cancelled, per a review dated Oct 2026 — [brandsearchreview.pro](https://brandsearchreview.pro/blog/brandsearch-free-trial/) (affiliate)
- The trial converts at the plan selected at signup. Cancel before day 3, because no prorated refund window is advertised — [brandsearchreview.pro](https://brandsearchreview.pro/blog/brandsearch-free-trial/)
- The trial includes ad spy filters, store analytics and the Spectre Chrome extension — [brandsearchreview.pro](https://brandsearchreview.pro/blog/brandsearch-free-trial/)
- Official terms: plans are month to month unless yearly, with no contract and no cancellation fee, and access is kept until the end of the paid period — [brandsearch.co/pricing](https://brandsearch.co/pricing)
- There is no free plan. A directory listing claiming a "free trial" ([toolsplorer.com](https://toolsplorer.com/tool/brandsearch/)) is loose wording for the $1 trial.

### Inferences
- With TrendTrack, a wrong purchase cannot be recovered. Monthly billing is the low-risk way to test before committing to annual.
- The Brandsearch $1 trial is a real but short evaluation window. Set a reminder to cancel within 72 hours if not continuing.

### Gaps
- Brandsearch's formal Terms of Service and refund clause were not retrieved.
- No source documents grandfathering or price-lock terms for either tool.

## Q4: Which plan does a supplement brand doing about €100K/month on Meta need, and what is the total cost?

### Takeaway
On TrendTrack, Pro is the realistic floor, because Starter's 2 tracked brands are too few for a competitive supplement niche. Pro costs about $89/mo or about $62/mo billed yearly (about $744/yr). Business ($149–159/mo, or about $111/mo yearly, about $1,332/yr) is needed if more than about 10 brands must be tracked under the Radar Ecom limits, or if 3 seats are needed. On Brandsearch, Outscaler is the sweet spot: $79/mo (about $948/yr) or about $59–63/mo annual (about $712–758/yr), with 50 tracked brands and 2 seats. These are inferences, not vendor recommendations.

### Cited Findings
- Plan limits and prices are cited above (Q1 and Q2).

### Inferences
- A €100K/mo Meta advertiser in supplements typically monitors about 10–30 competitors plus some adjacent DTC brands. It needs at least 2 seats (founder or media buyer plus a creative strategist). This is an assumption, not sourced.
- **TrendTrack:**
  - If Pro allows 30 tracked brands (majority sources), Pro at $89/mo ≈ $1,068/yr monthly-billed, or ≈ $744/yr at $62/mo yearly.
  - If Pro allows only 10 (Radar Ecom, Oct 2026), Business is needed at $149–159/mo ≈ $1,788–1,908/yr, or ≈ $1,332/yr at $111/mo yearly.
  - Business also covers 3 seats, while Pro is single-user. For a team, **Business is the likely real requirement**: about $1.3k–1.9k per year.
- **Brandsearch:**
  - Outscaler covers 50 brands and 2 seats: $79/mo ≈ $948/yr, or ≈ $712–758/yr on annual billing.
  - Agency ($149/mo ≈ $1,788/yr, or about $1,432/yr annual) is needed only for more than 2 seats or more than 50 brands.
- Both tools cost under 0.2% of monthly ad spend, so the price is negligible compared with the spend. The deciding factors should be data quality and coverage, not price.
- Euro-billed customers may see € pricing at the same nominal numbers (TrendTrack), and VAT may apply. This is not verified.

### Gaps
- No vendor guidance on plan choice by ad spend was found.
- VAT and € billing for EU customers were not confirmed for either tool.
